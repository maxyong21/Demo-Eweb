What is ready, and what comes next?

The demo includes shopping, sample checkout, order history, basic product management, customer viewing and interactive analytics. The requirements below show what still needs building. Sample products, reviews, policies and sales figures are not Lucky Dotcom's business records.

The scope, made clear

What each side needs.

“In demo” means a working browser-based example. “To build” means proposed work that is not implemented yet.

For your admin team

Less manual work. Clearer control.

  • In demoManage products and orders

    Edit prices and stock, view customers and update order status.

  • To buildUpload product photos

    Add multiple images, preview them, choose a cover photo, reorder or remove images, and edit specifications. Help buyers assess products with less messaging.

  • In demoApprove dealers and set wholesale rules

    Review applications and approve, decline or revoke dealer access. Set each product's minimum units, pack increments and quantity prices, plus an optional dealer order minimum. Retail customers remain unrestricted by dealer minimums.

  • To buildHandle quotations and payments

    Receive bulk enquiries, issue quotes and record acceptance. Verify payment separately from fulfilment and add actual tracking details.

  • In demoExplore order performance

    Filter by date and status, inspect daily results and categories, and export reports. Spot products and orders needing attention.

  • To buildMeasure profit and keep channels in sync

    Add product costs, campaign sources and promotion performance. Connect shared stock across the website, sales team and Shopee to reduce overselling.

For wholesale buyers

Less uncertainty. Easier ordering.

  • In demoBrowse and place a sample order

    Search products, adjust cart quantities, use sample checkout and view order history.

  • In demoApply for a dealer account

    Receive an optional invitation above 100 units, submit business details and wait for admin approval. Continue buying at retail prices while pending; dealer prices activate only after approval.

  • In demoUnderstand bulk prices before ordering

    See dealer minimums, pack increments and live quantity prices. Example: RM 1 retail; approved dealers pay RM 45 for 50 units or RM 85 for 100. Cart and checkout enforce the configured rules. Production delivery terms still need confirmation.

  • To buildOrder many products or request a quote

    Enter quantities in a quick order table, or submit one enquiry containing all products. Review and accept the admin team's quotation.

  • To buildBuy again with fewer steps

    Rebuild a previous order with current stock and pricing checks. Receive relevant stock alerts and reminders only after opting in.

  • To buildPay, track and get help

    Use connected payments, see payment and shipment updates, and send product-linked WhatsApp enquiries. Access clear warranty and return procedures.

One connected workflow

Dealer applies → admin approves and assigns prices → buyer orders or requests a quote → admin confirms payment and fulfils → buyer tracks and reorders.

Production also needs secure accounts, a shared database, image storage, staff permissions, backups and training. Scope and costs must be confirmed before implementation.

A few practical questions

Clear expectations
from the start.

Will a website automatically increase sales?

No. It can remove buying friction and improve follow-up, but it still needs suitable products, competitive offers, reliable service and a way to attract buyers. We test and measure these together.

Will we stop using Shopee?

No. Improve Shopee while building your direct channel through your own marketing and independently acquired enquiries. Keep Shopee transactions on Shopee.

Can our team manage the website?

The existing demo lets an administrator manage products, stock and orders. A production project should include staff access, training, backups, clear ownership and a maintenance arrangement.

Is this already connected to our business?

No. This is a browser-based demonstration with fictional catalogue and order data. Payments, inventory integrations and automated messaging require a separate implementation. Demo logins are for presentation only.

What should we prepare before starting?

Your verified product catalogue, wholesale rules, delivery and warranty terms, recent order and margin data, and details of the stock/accounting systems you use. These determine the scope and a realistic quotation.
